Keep captured raiders in front of a player distinct from raiders still in the public battle area. Keep the eight regions distinct from the towns inside them. A monastery doubles a town’s claim weight, not its regional-spread count.

## Source-sensitive decisions

- This supplied English edition always discards the final card, including with five players (p5). Do not silently substitute the different five-player rule mentioned in discussion of some other-language editions.
- Actions resolve by ascending number, independently of who wins. Only matching-colour or white cards qualify to win.
- Forced coin-loss symbols can substitute points; optional purchases cannot.
- Battle and church rewards recalculate leaders after the first reward removes the winner’s pieces. Battle’s zero-raider exclusion is explicit.
- A held claim survives a tied majority; half-value tied scoring applies to face-up tokens still on the board.
- The Princess’s military and trade benefits have different scopes; spurning discards the card.
- Source artwork inspected includes the red 11, red 2, white 13 and yellow 17 illustrated on p7. The PDF does not show all 25 card faces at readable size. The guide explains the complete icon system, not an invented card-by-card catalog. Marriage-track positions and individual marriage-card rewards are read from components.
