## Review focus and coverage boundary

- Preserved optional effects, the mandatory keycard-or-Recall choice, movement-effect continuity, and the post-recharge prohibition.
- Crystal conversion is asymmetric: 3 white → red, 3 red → purple, red → 2 white, purple → red + white. Icon verification used PDF p7 and the player board on p4.
- Follower pickup columns 1/2/2/3/3 and cube points 1/3/6/10/15 verified visually on p10.
- All 14 tribe descriptions on p15 and all 9 named rare-gadget clarifications on p14 are included in both languages. The PDF does not reproduce every physical gadget, tribe-card exchange table, keycard, upgrade side or objective combination. Do not infer unprinted values from a generic effect description.
- AYNIR exchange ratios and OMNITOOL’s individual printed options must be read from the actual components; only their rulebook clarifications were inspected. Workshop/monument costs that the prose refers to the player board remain explicitly tied to its printed costs.
- Optional companion is mentioned but not required or registered on the user’s behalf. No later errata, expansion or alternate-edition rulings were imported.
