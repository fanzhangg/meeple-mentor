# Age of Innovation player guide — rulebook audit

## Scope and result

Checked the Chinese and English player guide against the supplied English rulebook, version 1.1. The audit covers all 17 guide topics, the 81 faction/tile reference entries, all 14 checkpoint answers and explanations, and the 12 illustration captions. The seven Planning display exceptions are now also included under Income and development tracks.

No remaining rule mismatches were identified in this review. This is a manual review against the supplied edition, not a claim that every possible rules interaction has been formally verified. Later editions, external FAQs, errata, and the separate Automa rulebook are outside this audit.

## Corrections and clarifications applied

| Finding | Correction | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The development cost table only gave the normal Terraforming upgrade cost. A generic exception note was not enough to establish the Plains cost. | Both tables now explicitly give the Plains exception: 1 Tool, 1 Scholar and **1 Coin**, rather than 5 Coins. Added all seven Planning display exceptions. | pp. 14, 19; Plains cost icon visually inspected |
| Several Chinese Innovation entries lost their category heading when converted to individual reference entries. This obscured immediate, one-time timing. | Restored explicit immediate, one-time timing for all nine immediate-reward Innovations and all six additional-building Innovations. Kept their recurring income separately labeled Phase I. | p. 21 |
| Workshop availability was implied by taking a piece from the Planning display, but not stated as a requirement. | Explicitly require an available Workshop before building. | p. 12 |
| Chinese power cycling could be read as moving every Bowl I token regardless of the amount gained. | Explicitly state one token movement per point of power and loss of excess power when I and II are empty. | pp. 7–9 |
| Multiple-Spade wording and City building counts could be read too broadly or too narrowly. | Clarified the 2/3 free-Spade effect and stated **at least** four buildings for ordinary City formation. Added the prohibition on supplementing Science-bonus Spades or building with them. | pp. 9, 11–12, 17 |
| Two-player information only referred to setup elsewhere. | Added non-player Scholar placement, Level-2 starting markers, the setup-only advances from Rounds 1–5, the Key exception, and the meaning of the Area Score tile. | p. 19 |

## Quiz and illustration checks

All 14 existing correct-answer indices remain unchanged in both languages. The checked cases include adjacency versus Sailing, three-power offers costing two points, power cycling, multi-Spade Reach restrictions, University City thresholds, Competencies versus Innovations, Keys at Level 8, permanent Scholars, the Palace surcharge, public actions versus conversions, Passing, Round 6 and Annexes in Area scoring.

The 12 captions agree with the depicted examples and underlying rules: building upgrade paths; two-Spade sequencing; Mountain-to-Desert cost; power cycle and neighboring builds; adjacency/Reach; Guild discounts; City formation; Science thresholds; Innovation costs; power sacrifice; and final-round scoring icons. Images remain artwork-only crops; page-reference labels were not restored to the player page.

## Validation

- Direct PDF text checks plus visual inspection of icon-dependent costs and rewards.
- Both language versions retain matching topic IDs, question IDs, correct-answer indices, tables, illustrations and reference-entry counts.
- Seven automated tests pass, including checks for the audit corrections. Automated tests protect content structure and known facts; they do not replace the manual rules comparison.
- English and Chinese guide data pass JavaScript syntax checks.
